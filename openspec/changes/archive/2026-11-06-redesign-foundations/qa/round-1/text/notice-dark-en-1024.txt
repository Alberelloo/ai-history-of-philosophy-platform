Before you start

PhiloGraph is a textbook on the history of philosophy: source chapters, cards for concepts and people, a graph of how ideas originated, and an assistant that answers with links to paragraphs.

Everything the model wrote without support from sources is marked with an icon and a frame: an example of a generated line. Such lines are the model’s suggestion, not an established fact.

Generation without sources is on. You can turn it off here or in settings.

Questions to the assistant, card descriptions and graph extension are processed by an external language model provider under its own rules. Do not include personal data in questions.

The model adds descriptions and links; such places are marked.
